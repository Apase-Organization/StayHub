# TSK-100 — read-only eligible room offer search

Status: ACCEPTED
Reviewer: Controller scoped source/diff review; acceptance limited to the recorded offer-read API.

## Delivered scope

- `POST /api/v1/properties/{propertyId}/availability` returns a single property
  containing its active room-type/rate-plan offers, rather than duplicating cards.
- Dates use `[checkIn, checkOut)`. Offer availability is the minimum stock across
  every requested night after OOO and HELD/CONFIRMED commitments. Shared plans read
  the same type/day pool; a plan stop-sale only hides that plan. Missing calendar
  rows fail closed.
- Every per-room adults/child-age allocation is checked against the type; payment
  mode must be allowed by the plan.
- Without dates, live quantity and total stay are null. Dated total basis is room
  price × rooms × nights plus one cleaning fee; taxes are explicitly disabled.
- No reservation is created; availability is advisory and requires CSRF as a POST.
